## 6. Fix before publishing

Four settled against the actual sources:

1. **Shannon ran in two parts.** 27(3) July *and* 27(4) October 1948. Citing 27(3) alone covers half the paper.
2. **Wiener's 1948 first edition is Wiley, not MIT Press.** MIT Press did the 1961 second edition. The current citation conflates them.
3. **Aristotle II.1 checks out verbatim** — "good sense, good moral character, and goodwill." Beat 49's "three stats" is sound.
4. **Kant's transcendental formula is in Appendix II**, as cited.

Three still open:

5. **Conway's volume/issue is unresolved.** His own site gives only "April 1968"; secondary sources split between 14(4) and 14(5). Cite month, year and his page — don't assert an issue number.
6. **Cialdini 1984 needs a print check** if anything is quoted from it. See §3.
7. **Weber vs. Antonakis on beat 3 is a deliberate conflict** — Weber held charisma could not be taught; Antonakis tested the opposite. Stage it as old view versus evidence.

Say "borrowing from Shannon" rather than "Shannon showed" on beats 12, 28, 6, 44 and 5.
Those are analogies, not evidence, and a technical audience will notice.
