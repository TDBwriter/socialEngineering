## Charisma Builds

**Social Engineering for Influence and Self-Defense at Enterprise Scale**

A concept in development. The argument: charisma is a learnable build rather than an
issued trait, social engineering is a neutral set of influence techniques, and engineers
need them in both directions — to lead without authority, and to recognize when the
techniques are aimed at them. Generative AI raises the stakes on both sides by making
polished output cheap, which leaves judgment, trust and verification as the scarce goods.

Every claim is traced to the person who first stated the idea. That citation discipline
is the project's defining constraint, not decoration.

## The numbering rule

The beats **1–52** in `outline.md` are the spine. The bibliography, the lightning cut and
the book map all address beats by number. Renumbering breaks every cross-reference in the
repo. If a beat is cut, retire its number rather than reusing it.
