# Bibliography

Origin sources for *Charisma Builds*. The governing rule of this project: **wherever
possible, each claim is traced to the person who first stated the idea.** The "Why it's
the origin" column records that justification and should survive into the book.

Slide numbers refer to the beats in [`outline.md`](outline.md).

## Notes

- **Analogies, not evidence:** Shannon (slides 12 and 28), Wiener (6), Turing (44) and Kant (5) are the original sources of the *concept*. You are applying each by analogy. Say "borrowing from Shannon" on the slide rather than "Shannon showed," because a technical audience will notice.
- **Where the modern source is the original:** Slides 3, 42, 48 and 52 make empirical claims that only recent studies support. I kept one modern source on each because nothing older makes the claim.
- **Possible conflict on slide 3:** Weber thought charisma was an extraordinary quality that could not be taught. Citing him next to Antonakis lets you show the old view and the evidence that challenges it on one slide.
- **Verification:** These citations come from memory, not a live search. Check editions, volume/issue numbers and translations against the sources before publishing. For older works, the year shown is first publication.
