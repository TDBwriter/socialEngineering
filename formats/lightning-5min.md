# Format: 5-Minute Lightning Talk

Status: **proposal, not the author's cut.** Two competing strategies are below with a
recommendation. Beat numbers refer to [`outline.md`](../outline.md).

---

## The arithmetic

Five minutes is **roughly 650–750 spoken words**, or **8 beats at 37 seconds each**.
For comparison, the 60-minute version gives each beat 58 seconds. A lightning talk is
not the 60-minute talk at 12× speed; it is a different talk that happens to share a
thesis.

## Strategy A — Compress the thesis (the trailer)

Take the load-bearing chain and drop everything else. Eight beats:

| # | Beat | Job in the 5 minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every technical system is wrapped in a social system you never read the source for | Hook. Establishes why an engineering audience should care. |
| 3 | Charisma is a build you spec into, not a trait you were issued | The promise. This is the title claim. |
| 2 | "Social engineering" is a neutral tool; attackers borrowed it from salespeople | The permission structure for everything after. |
| 16 | Specificity is charisma: vague asks get vague outcomes | One mechanic, proving the build is real and giving a takeaway. |
| 35 | Urgency, authority, isolation: the recipe for every manipulation you'll face | One defense, covering the other half of the title. |
| 48 | The skills AI cannot commoditize are the ones this talk is about | Why now. |
| 51 | If it only works when they don't know you're doing it, don't do it | The ethical floor. Non-negotiable; never cut. |
| 52 | Competence gets you in the room; charisma determines whether the room does anything about it | Close. |

**Trim to 7** by folding beat 2's reframe into beat 3 as a single sentence.

**What this costs:** all of Act IV (peer leadership) and Act V (managing up) — which is
the most practically useful material in the whole outline — plus the entire systems
argument of Act II. The audience leaves with the thesis and no tools.

## Strategy B — Vertical slice (one act, done properly)

Take a single act and give it the full five minutes. The strongest candidate is **Act VI,
Self-Defense**, as *"Social Engineering: The Defense Is the Interesting Half."*

| # | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 34 | The same techniques that make you effective make you a target | Hook, and it implies the rest of the thesis without arguing for it. |
| 35 | Urgency, authority, isolation | The recipe. The memorable unit. |
| 37 | Weaponized vagueness moves accountability onto you | The one most people have experienced and never named. |
| 39 | "I'll get back to you" is a complete sentence and a security control | The takeaway they repeat to a colleague afterwards. |
| 47 | Verification rituals must now be out-of-band by default | Why now (AI), in one beat. |
| 51 | The ethical floor | Close on the ethics; it reads as earned rather than tacked on. |

Six beats at ~50 seconds each. Room to tell one real story.

## Recommendation

**Strategy B.** A lightning audience retains one idea, not eight. Strategy A produces a
competent trailer for a talk nobody in the room is going to see; Strategy B produces a
talk. It also leaves the full thesis intact as the thing the 60-minute version and the
book are *for*, instead of spending it in five minutes.

If the lightning slot is being used as promotion for the long talk or the book, invert
the judgment and use Strategy A — a trailer is the correct artifact in that case.

Either way, **beat 51 stays in.** A five-minute talk on influence technique with the
ethical floor cut out is a different and worse talk.
